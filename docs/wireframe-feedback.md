## Design Decisions

| Suggestion | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Rename Study Hours to Study/Work Hours or Work Hours | Deferred | Recovery Compass v0.1 is designed for high-school students, and the field currently represents academic workload. Broadening it to employment would change the target user, analytics, and feedback interpretation. Reconsider after v1.0 if the product expands beyond students. |
